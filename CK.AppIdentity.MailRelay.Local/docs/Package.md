The Local side of the CK-AppIdentity "MailRelay" feature: a local party that declares "MailRelay" sends the mails of its
remote parties with its own CK.Mailer senders (`LocalMailRelayFeatureDriver`, `MailRelayHandler` of the `ISendMailCommand`). See
https://github.com/signature-opensource/CK-Mailer/blob/stable/CK.AppIdentity.MailRelay.Local/README.md.
