The Local side of the CK-AppIdentity "MailRelay" feature: a local party that declares "MailRelay" sends the mails of its
remote parties with its own CK.Mailer senders.

Main types: `LocalMailRelayFeatureDriver` and `MailRelayHandler`, the handler of the `ISendMailCommand`.

See the [README](https://github.com/signature-opensource/CK-Mailer/blob/stable/CK.AppIdentity.MailRelay.Local/README.md).
