The Remote side of the CK-AppIdentity "MailSender" feature: a local party sends its mails through its MailSender party,
without any mail configuration of its own (`RemoteMailSenderFeatureDriver`, the "MailSender" sender of CK.Mailer:
`MailSenderEmailSenderFactory`). See
https://github.com/signature-opensource/CK-Mailer/blob/stable/CK.AppIdentity.MailSender.Remote/README.md.
