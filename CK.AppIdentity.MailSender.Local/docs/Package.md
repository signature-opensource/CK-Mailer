The Local side of the CK-AppIdentity "MailSender" feature: a local party that allows "MailSender" sends the mails of its
remote parties with its own CK.Mailer configuration (`LocalMailSenderFeatureDriver`, `MailSenderHandler` of the `ISendMailCommand`). See
https://github.com/signature-opensource/CK-Mailer/blob/stable/CK.AppIdentity.MailSender.Local/README.md.
