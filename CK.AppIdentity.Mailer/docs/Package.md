The Local side of the CK-AppIdentity "MailSender" feature: the application sends the mails of its remote parties with its
own CK.Mailer configuration (`LocalMailSenderFeatureDriver`, `MailSenderHandler` of the `ISendMailCommand`). See
https://github.com/signature-opensource/CK-Mailer/blob/stable/CK.AppIdentity.Mailer/README.md.
